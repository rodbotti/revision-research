About

[Re]vision Research

[Re]vision Research is a quarterly publication dedicated to tracking the Brazilian Insurance-Linked Securities (ILS) and reinsurance market. Developed by seasoned industry professionals, it offers technical analysis, up-to-date data and independent perspectives.

Drawing on public SUSEP data and proprietary methodologies, each edition presents rankings of cedents and reinsurers, income statements, key metrics and specialist articles on regulatory and structural market topics.

The publication is sponsored by ABECOR — the Brazilian Association of Reinsurance Brokerage Firms (Associação Brasileira das Empresas de Corretagem de Resseguros).
